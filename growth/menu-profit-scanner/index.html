How the recoverable number is worked out

  1. Net price is the menu price divided by 1.05 when prices include GST. Plate cost is grossed up by the wastage percentage, so food cost % = plate cost × (1 + wastage) ÷ net price.
  2. Plowhorse: price is lifted in ₹5 steps until the item reaches the menu's average rupee margin, capped at +15%, and 6% of its volume is assumed lost. Where trimming the plate cost to your target food cost pays more, that number is used instead. Only the larger of the two is counted, never both.
  3. Puzzle: a 25% volume lift from card placement, a staff upsell line and a photo. This is the softest assumption here — treat it as a test, not a forecast.
  4. Dog: delisted, with 30% of its covers moving to your highest-margin Star.
  5. Wastage: bringing spoilage down to 3% of plate cost, which is where a café with daily indent discipline lands.
  6. Every figure is an estimate built on the numbers you typed. It is a conversation starter for a priced decision, not an audited projection — and it assumes your plate costs are honest.
